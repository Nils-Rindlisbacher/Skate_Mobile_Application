Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 17, 2024

Welcome to Skaterz. We respect your privacy and want to be transparent about how we handle your data.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account Information: We collect your username, email, and password (encrypted) to provide you with an account.
  • Profile Data: You may optionally upload a profile picture and provide your name.
  • Skating Data: We store the tricks you log, your session history, and equipment setup.

2. User Generated Content (UGC) & Safety

Zero Tolerance Policy: Skaterz has a zero-tolerance policy for abusive content. This includes offensive profile pictures, inappropriate usernames, or harassment via the leaderboard. Users can be reported by others and will be permanently banned if they violate these safety guidelines.

3. Social Features

If your profile is set to Public, other users can see your username, profile picture, and skating progress on the Leaderboard. You can switch your profile to Private at any time in the Settings menu.

4. Data Deletion

We believe you should have full control over your data. You can delete your entire account and all associated data (tricks, sessions, images) at any time via the Settings > Delete Account button within the app.

5. Third-Party Services

We do not sell your data. We use standard secure storage and hosting services to keep the app running.
